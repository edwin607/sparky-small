Lesson 2.3: Opportunity Cost

Every choice costs more than money — it costs what you gave up. In this lesson you'll learn to spot the hidden price tag on everyday decisions and use it to make better ones.

Your goal: explain opportunity cost in your own words and apply it to a real decision from your life. Sparky will coach you there — it won't hand you the answer.
